# Swag Labs - Requirements

REQ-LOGIN-01: A user with valid credentials shall reach the inventory page.
REQ-LOGIN-02: A user with an incorrect password shall be refused with an error message.
REQ-LOGIN-03: A user who submits an empty username shall be told the username is required.
REQ-LOGIN-04: A locked-out account shall be told it is locked out, not that the password is wrong.
REQ-CART-01: Adding a product shall increase the cart badge by one.
REQ-CART-02: The product shown in the cart shall be the product that was added.
REQ-CHECKOUT-01: A customer who supplies a first name, last name and postcode shall reach the order confirmation.
REQ-CHECKOUT-02: Checkout shall be refused when the postcode is missing.
